Project 02 · OpenDSS

24-Hour Grid Stress Analysis of a German LV Feeder

Modeled a German 400V feeder from scratch — transformer, underground cabling, and eight connected households — then ran an hour-by-hour power flow using synthetic load, EV charging, and solar profiles.

Key finding: Midday solar export pushes net load negative, while early evening — when EV charging and household demand overlap with no solar left — is the critical stress window.
OpenDSSPythonTime-Series
Project 03 · pandapower

Hosting Capacity Study of a Rural German LV Grid

Used a benchmark rural low-voltage network to test how much EV charging and solar PV the grid can absorb before voltage limits are violated.

Key finding: Rooftop solar causes overvoltage problems at lower capacity than EV charging causes undervoltage, since the rural feeder's baseline voltage already sits close to the upper limit.
pandapowerSimBenchPython

Solar Parking Lot with EV Charging and Battery Storage
